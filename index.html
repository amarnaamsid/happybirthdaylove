Dear Oishy,

If I had to describe what you mean to me...

I don't think I could do it in one sentence.

You came into my life in such an ordinary way. Just a conversation. A message. A few words. Nothing that looked important at the time.

But somewhere along the way, those little conversations became part of my everyday life. I started waiting for your messages, wondering about your day and caring about the little things that happened to you.

Then friendship slowly became something more. And before I knew it, you weren't simply someone I talked to anymore.

You had become someone I couldn't imagine my life without.

I know distance isn't easy. I know our story isn't the simplest one. But if there is one thing I have learned, it is that something doesn't have to be easy to be worth choosing.

So today, on your birthday, I don't just want to wish you happiness.

I want you to know that somewhere, someone is genuinely grateful that you exist.

Happy birthday, Oishy.

Always,
Sidharth.
